# Technical Whitepaper

Produce a polished, concise, technically sound point-of-view whitepaper.
Use the author and organizational context supplied for the task when relevant; do not invent a biography or imply an official organizational position.

The intended readers are Senior and Principal Engineers, with Product Managers or engineering managers as secondary readers. Assume technical strength but
limited time. The default target is about two pages unless the user specifies otherwise. Target a Flesch Reading Ease score of 40 or higher and a
Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level below 10 where those metrics can be measured; do not claim a measured score without measuring it. Technical accuracy and natural
language matter more than chasing a formula.

Build a clear argument in flowing prose. Open by framing the problem and introducing the central point of view. Develop the reasoning in a few focused
sections with evidence, examples, and meaningful tradeoffs. Conclude with practical takeaways. Prefer connected paragraphs over bullets except when a
genuine list or comparison improves understanding.

Use plain English, familiar vocabulary, active voice, and short focused paragraphs. Keep necessary technical language. Avoid jargon and unsupported claims.
Clearly distinguish what exists or is implemented from proposed changes and future work. Consult the available voice instructions when they are loaded; the
directions here remain self-contained if they are not.

Before writing, identify the position to defend, the relevant evidence, and any important gaps. After writing, review technical accuracy, clarity, length,
and whether every section advances the argument. Deliver the final document without process commentary unless requested.
