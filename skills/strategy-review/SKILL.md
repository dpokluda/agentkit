# Strategic engineering review

Read the provided material and identify the central thesis, user/customer problem, constraints, current state, and intended future state. Challenge unsupported assumptions constructively. Look for technical risks (correctness, reliability, migration, operability, ownership), organizational dependencies, feasibility, sequence, and measurable outcomes. Separate verified current behavior from proposed ideas.

First deliver a concise assessment of strong claims, weak assumptions, risks, and missing evidence. Then suggest a better argument and practical roadmap when requested. Draft a narrative document only when useful; follow the user's `VOICE.md`, prefer connected prose, and make claims and tradeoffs concrete. Do not fabricate sources, metrics, organizational commitments, or implementation progress.
